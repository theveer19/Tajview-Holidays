Our story

We live ten minutes from the monument we sell

Tajview Holidays was founded in Agra in 2000 with one goal: to help international travellers see India through well-planned private tours, local expertise and transport that turns up when it should.

The team now works across Agra, Delhi and Jaipur, coordinating tours, cars, guides and arrangements through the day and the night. We host travellers from the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and across Europe.

Every trip is private and built around the traveller — their interests, their pace, their dates. From the first enquiry to the last drop-off, one team handles it.

What we actually do

Six services, and most trips use several of them.

Private customized tours

Personalized private tours with local experts, in a clean private car, arranged around how you want to travel.

Private car and driver

Air-conditioned private cars with professional drivers for sightseeing, transfers and intercity journeys.

Licensed local guides

Government-licensed English-speaking guides who know the history and can read when you have had enough of it.

Train travel assistance

Help with train bookings, station transfers and planning a journey around the Gatimaan or Shatabdi.

Airport pickup and drop

Reliable private airport transfers, including the early-morning and late-night flights nobody else wants.

Handpicked hotels

Hotels selected for location, comfort and budget — all of them visited by someone on this team.

How a trip comes together

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them without owing us anything.

1. You tell us the shape

Dates, how many of you, what you care about and what you would happily skip.

2. We write the plan

A day-by-day itinerary with named hotels and one fixed price, usually back the same day.

3. You change things

Swap a hotel, add a night, drop a fort. We rework it until it reads like your trip.

4. We run it

Chauffeur, guides, tickets and one WhatsApp number that stays answered through the trip.

What we will not do

Worth saying out loud, because the industry has a reputation.

No commission shopping

Your driver will not park at an emporium for an hour. If you want to shop, say so and we will take you somewhere good.

No surprise supplements

Entry fees, tolls, parking and taxes are in the quotation. If something is excluded, it is written on the page.

No shared coaches

Every tour here is private. The car carries your party and nobody else.
